Atom 118 privacy policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Controller

  • Developer and controller: David Vilela Freire (individual developer)
  • Country: Spain
  • Contact email: siegethegame@protonmail.com
  • Application: Atom 118 (iOS com.davidvilelafreire.atom118; Android com.davidvilelafreire.atom118)
  • Paid product: com.davidvilelafreire.atom118.fullgame

2. Summary

Atom 118 is a single-player game that works offline. It does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs. Everything the game needs to remember (settings, high score and the game in progress) is stored only on your device.

3. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, request, store on servers, transmit or share with anyone:

  • name, email address, phone number, address or other contact information;
  • accounts, passwords, sign-ins or user identifiers;
  • IP address, device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) or device fingerprints;
  • location (approximate or precise);
  • contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or clipboard;
  • usage data, events, diagnostics, crashes or logs;
  • payment data, cards or financial information;
  • health data, biometric data or special categories of data.

The game makes no network calls of its own to work. On Android the package declares the permissions INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and com.android.vending.BILLING, which the Google Play Billing library adds to handle purchases (that traffic is carried out by Google Play and contains no game data), and VIBRATE, for the haptic vibration of the arcade mode (it collects no data). On iOS the app requests no permission that touches personal data.

We do not profile, track you across apps or sites, or show personalized advertising. For that reason the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is not shown either: there is nothing to track.

4. What is stored and where (local storage)

Atom 118 stores a minimal set of game data in the app's private storage (user://):

  • Settings: music, sound effects, mode (Normal/practice) and language.
  • High score: best score, in Normal mode only.
  • Game in progress: a recovery state (atom, sequence, instability, radioactive encounter, clock and checkpoint of the arcade mode) saved at safe points and when the app is suspended.
  • It is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties (operating-system backups may include it).
  • It is not linked to any identity: there are no accounts or identifiers.
  • It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data from the system settings, except for backups you have made.

We do not use cookies, pixels, server-side storage or cloud backups controlled by the developer.

5. In-app purchases

Unlocking the full game is a one-time, non-consumable purchase (com.davidvilelafreire.atom118.fullgame). There are no subscriptions, virtual currency, consumables or random purchases.

  • Payment is processed by Apple (App Store/StoreKit) on iOS and by Google (Google Play/Play Billing) on Android.
  • The developer does not receive or see payment data (card, billing or the buyer's identity).
  • The app only asks the store (through Apple or Google Play) whether the purchase is unlocked and keeps that state locally. It does not create a purchase profile or send it to any developer server.
  • Apple and Google process the data needed for the purchase as independent controllers, under their own policies: Apple · Google.

6. Children (COPPA / CalOPPA and GDPR/EU)

Atom 118 is suitable for all audiences and is not specifically directed at children, nor does it knowingly collect information from children.

  • COPPA (US, under 13): the rule applies when a service collects personal information from children. Atom 118 does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13; therefore there is no collection, use or disclosure that would require direct notice or verifiable parental consent.
  • CalOPPA (California): we do not collect personally identifiable information from California residents. We still publish this policy in an accessible way with an effective date, and we do not track activity over time or across sites or apps.
  • GDPR (EU/EEA): because we do not process players' personal data, no consent or age verification is required to use the game.

Parents or guardians can write to siegethegame@protonmail.com; there is no child's data to review or delete because none is collected.

7. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Atom 118 does not process personal data, so no legal basis under Article 6 applies to the normal use of the game. The only exception is if you write to support: the email and its content will be processed on the basis of consent (Art. 6.1.a) or of the legitimate interest in answering your query (Art. 6.1.f), solely to reply and for as long as necessary.

8. Your rights

Although we hold no personal data about you, if you are in the EEA or in a jurisdiction with equivalent rights you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw consent, by writing to siegethegame@protonmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Spain, the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

9. Security and retention

  • There is no data on developer servers: there is no database to protect against breaches.
  • Local data relies on the operating system's protections (storage encryption when enabled).
  • Retention: local data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. For purchases, retention is handled by Apple and Google.

10. Third parties and links

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect data. The only third parties involved are the stores, which process purchases under their own policies (section 5). The links to Apple and Google are provided for reference only.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, the stores or the law change. We will publish the new version at the same URL with a new effective date. If a change involved collecting data, we will say so prominently and, where the law requires it, before applying it.
