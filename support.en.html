Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase? Open the game, tap PLAY and then the gear (top right) → “Unlock full game” → “Restore purchase”. It is also on the unlock screen at the end of the demo. No account needed. (If you already own the full game, that button does not appear.)

Does the game collect data? No. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Everything is stored only on your device. See the Privacy policy.

Does the game need a connection? Not to play. The store handles the purchase or its restoration.
